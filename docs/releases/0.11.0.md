The first release of Iris, a Matrix client built on Cinny, for the web and Android.

### New

- Home has sections: Starred and your own, each mixing chats and rooms
- A message you start on one device waits in the composer on your other devices
- Scheduled messages: pick a time, and your homeserver sends the message then
- On Android, notifications arrive while the app is closed, through UnifiedPush
- Crop, rotate and draw on an image before you send it
- Add stickers to a pack, and manage your packs, from the sticker board
- The Iris light and Iris dark themes
